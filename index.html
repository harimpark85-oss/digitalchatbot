A Media Archaeology of Digital Language

Artificial Languages

Every communication technology has its own architecture, and it intervenes in the way we communicate.

Yet people creatively push beyond these limits, inventing digital languages that exceed both the conventions of natural language and the technology’s original purposes. Far from being imperfect substitutes, these languages open up new possibilities for expression, play, and intimacy.

Choose a chatbot built on one of these artificial languages, from the pager to generative AI, and start a conversation. Then explore our assumptions and ideologies about intimacy and human language.

Decode appears under replies in every mode except Generative AI. It reveals meaning and mechanism, and keeps three kinds of expression apart:

  • documented a historically documented Korean digital expression
  • literal translation a word-for-word English translation of a Korean convention
  • experimental a new expression the AI invented

An artistic experiment inspired by Korean digital language history. Every reply is generated live by Claude; English English replies translate Korean conventions literally, so they may read oddly on purpose, and the AI's labels can be wrong. In pager mode, any code not on the reference list is marked with * and labelled experimental automatically.
